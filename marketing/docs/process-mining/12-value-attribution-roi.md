# Process Mining — Value Attribution, ROI & Business Economics

## 1. Value Attribution Framework

### Celonis "Path to Value" Methodology

Three phases: **Frame → Build → Realize**

1. **Framing**: Identify value opportunities tied to specific KPIs. Each gets a baseline from prior fiscal year or trailing 12 months.
2. **Building**: Implement process improvements, automations, action flows.
3. **Realizing & Tracking**: Transformation Hub calculates KPI improvements daily, converting delta into dollar value.

**Formula**: `Realized Value = (KPI improvement from baseline) × (monetary conversion factor)`

**Example**: Cash discount capture improves from 61% → 90% on $500M spend with 2% average discount = ~$2.9M additional captured discounts.

**Critical**: Celonis requires the customer's **finance department to sign off** that value has been realized — not just identified. This is the "Problem-Solution-Outcome" framework linking operational data to P&L.

**Scale**: 120 "Value Champions" have realized **>$10M each**, totaling **$8.1 billion** in verified value. $18B+ identified across all customers.

## 3. Value Levers — Specific Impact

### Cash Discount Capture
- **Typical impact**: 1-3% of addressable spend
- Deutsche Telekom: 96% capture rate = EUR 40M/year
- Fresenius Kabi: 40% → 97%
- **Mechanism**: Real-time alerts on blocked invoices, automated payment scheduling

### Duplicate Payment Prevention
- **Typical impact**: 0.1-0.5% of total AP spend
- Deutsche Telekom: EUR 3M
- Industrial manufacturer: $620K recovered/quarter + $300K/quarter prevention
- **Mechanism**: Cross-referencing all outgoing payments, flagging duplicates

### Maverick Spend Reduction
- **Typical impact**: 2-5% reduction in off-contract spending
- Global medical device company: >$1M in 3 months
- **Mechanism**: Identifying offenders, notifying category managers, blocking maverick POs

### DSO Reduction
- **Typical impact**: 5-20 day improvement
- GE Healthcare: 18 days removed
- Lab instrument manufacturer: DSO halved
- **Mechanism**: Collections prioritization, delinquent account ranking, automated dunning

### DPO / Working Capital Optimization
- **Typical impact**: 10-32 day DPO improvement
- IQVIA: +32 days DPO, $600K freed
- French automotive: EUR 33M working capital impact
- **Mechanism**: Payment term alignment, elimination of early payments

### Automation Rate Improvement
- **Typical impact**: 20-50 percentage point increase in STP

### Cycle Time Reduction
- **Typical impact**: 20-57% reduction

- **Mechanism**: Bottleneck identification, unnecessary step elimination

### Compliance Improvement
- **Typical impact**: 20-25% improvement

## 5. Building the Business Case

### What Convinces the CFO

1. **Current state quantification**: PM creates an objective baseline showing exactly where money is lost — actual data, not estimates
2. **Value at stake**: Specific dollars tied to identifiable inefficiencies ("We miss $X in cash discounts, pay $Y in duplicates, lose $Z in early payments")
3. **Quick wins**: 3-5 improvements achievable in first 90 days
4. **Before/after proof**: Re-run analysis after improvements to show quantified delta
5. **Scaling roadmap**: Show how initial process extends to 5-10+

### Key Metrics for CFOs
- Working capital freed (DSO/DPO/DIO impact)
- Cost per transaction reduction
- STP rate improvement
- Cash discount capture rate
- Compliance rate improvement (penalty avoidance)
- FTE capacity freed (usually redeployment, not headcount reduction)

### What Companies Expect (Deloitte 2025 Survey)
- Process improvements: 61%
- Cost savings: 59% (up from 46%)
- Process transparency: 51%
- Process monitoring: 41%
- Automation identification: 38%

## 7. Failure Economics

### What Goes Wrong
1. **Unrealistic expectations**: First project too ambitious in scope
2. **Too slow to show value**: Undermines organizational belief in ROI
3. **Cultural damage**: A failed project kills PM attempts for years
4. **Data quality**: Incomplete/inaccurate logs distort insights
5. **Lack of management support**: Cited by 41% (Deloitte 2025, up from 26% in 2021)
6. **Budget constraints**: Cited by 24%

### Cost of Failure
- Direct: Wasted licenses ($100K-$1M+), consulting ($200K-$500K+), internal FTE costs
- Indirect: Organizational skepticism, lost opportunity cost, delayed transformation
- **Recommendation**: Start small, connect to specific business driver, show results within 8 weeks
