# Process Mining Market Reality Research
## Hard Data, Real Voices, Adoption Barriers & Buyer Concerns

*Compiled: March 2026 from public surveys, analyst reports, user reviews, and academic research*

---

## 1. DELOITTE GLOBAL PROCESS MINING SURVEY 2025

**Source:** [Deloitte Global Process Mining Survey 2025](https://www.deloitte.com/de/de/services/consulting-financial/research/global-process-mining-survey.html) | [6 Takeaways - PEX Network](https://www.processexcellencenetwork.com/process-mining/articles/6-takeaways-deloitte-global-process-mining-survey-2025)

**Sample:** 120+ respondents across three segments: enterprise-wide implementers, standalone deployers, and proof-of-concept phase organizations.

### Key Adoption Barrier Statistics (with YoY trends)

| Barrier | 2025 | 2021 | Trend |
|---------|------|------|-------|
| Management support / buy-in | **41%** | 26% | UP (alarming) |
| Lack of organizational priority | **29%** | 48% | DOWN (improving) |
| Budget constraints | **24%** | 26% | FLAT |

**Interpretation:** Front-line employees increasingly see the value, but management is NOT buying in. The gap has flipped -- the bottleneck moved from awareness to executive commitment.

### Adoption Scale
- **48%** of organizations report company-wide adoption (up from 45% in 2021) -- growth, but glacially slow
- **25%** currently use AI with process mining
- **74%** plan to include AI in future initiatives

### Expectations Shift
- **61%** expect process improvements (down from 77% in 2021)
- **59%** expect cost savings (up from 46% in 2021)

### Four Critical Levers Identified
1. Centers of Excellence
2. Management buy-in
3. AI and automation extensions
4. Continuous process optimization

## 3. GARTNER DATA

**Sources:** [Gartner Forecast Analysis](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/4007520) | [2025 Magic Quadrant - PEX Network](https://www.processexcellencenetwork.com/process-mining/articles/highlights-gartners-2025-magic-quadrant-process-mining)

### Market Penetration
- **By 2026, only 25% of global enterprises** will have embraced process mining platforms as a first step to creating a digital twin for business operations (Gartner projection)
- **80% of organizations** plan to integrate process mining into at least 10% of their business operations
- Market grew to **$2.3 billion by 2025** with a **33% CAGR**
- Worldwide spending grew **30%+ in 2024** (following 39.5% in 2023)

**Key insight:** The market is growing fast in dollars, but **75% of enterprises still haven't adopted it at all** as of the Gartner projection.

## 5. DATA PREPARATION: THE HIDDEN TIME SINK

**Sources:** [AltExSoft](https://www.altexsoft.com/blog/process-mining/) | [McCoy Partners](https://mccoy-partners.com/en/updates/waarom-datavoorbereiding-de-sleutel-tot-succes-is-bij-elk-process-mining-project) | [IBM Research]

| Source | Data Prep % of Project Time |
|--------|---------------------------|
| IBM | **40-70%** |
| Multiple academic sources | **60-90%** |
| Most frequently cited benchmark | **~80%** |
| Eindhoven University / Fluxicon | **80%** for new projects |

**What this means:** If a process mining project takes 6 months, **4-5 months are spent just getting the data ready.** The actual analysis is a fraction of the effort. This is why projects feel like they take forever before delivering any value.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION COSTS & TIMELINES

**Source:** [ProcessMaker Pricing Guide](https://www.processmaker.com/blog/how-much-does-process-mining-cost-2024-pricing-guide/)

### License Costs
| Tier | Annual Cost Range |
|------|------------------|
| Platform license (typical) | **$5,000 - $150,000/year** |
| Center of Excellence setup | **$1M+** |
| Celonis (reported range) | **$15,000 - $200,000+/year** |
| Celonis (comprehensive enterprise) | **~$100,000+/year** commonly reported |
| IBM Process Mining | **$3,200/month** (1 analyst, 20M events) |
| Proof of Concept (typical) | **<$60,000 per process** |

### Implementation Costs (Beyond License)
- **$50,000-$100,000** of work-related cost per process in Year 1
- Implementations typically require **12-24 months** and **multiple FTEs**
- Running costs increase **3-5x** as more processes are covered

### Time to Value
- Best case (targeted pilot): **4-8 weeks** to first insights
- Typical enterprise deployment: **12-24 months** for full rollout
- Most enterprise leaders expect incremental value in **2-6 month cycles**

## 7. CELONIS-SPECIFIC COMPLAINTS (FROM REAL USER REVIEWS)

**Sources:** [G2 Reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/celonis/reviews) | [PeerSpot](https://www.peerspot.com/products/celonis-reviews) | [Celonis Community](https://community.celonis.com/general-product-q-a-2/is-celonis-becoming-too-complicated-4854)

### Pricing Complaints
- "Celonis is expensive and not for mid or low-sized enterprises because the license price is high"
- "Monthly licensing fees are extremely expensive -- $150 per month just to view existing dashboards"
- "The licensing structure is always changing, with all newest capabilities always being add-ons for existing contracts"
- "Licensing and pricing strategies need adjustments for more affordability, particularly in markets like Ukraine and India"
- "The entry-level is focused on enterprise-size companies, and for the SMB segment it is out of their range"
- "Flexible cost models would help smaller players adapt Celonis into their business operations"

### Complexity / Learning Curve Complaints
- "The learning curve can be a bit steep at the start, especially when you want to build more complex analyses or customize dashboards"
- "Some features feel hidden behind too many clicks, and occasionally performance slows down when handling very large datasets"
- "Complexity is the biggest problem"
- "For a new user, the interface can feel heavy and complex"
- "Users express frustration with the complexity of Celonis, noting a steep learning curve and unnecessary features"
- "Celonis could be simpler for the business user -- simplifying it even more would be beneficial"

### PQL-Specific Complaints
- "PQL is a code that only Celonis uses, so there is little help on the internet or through AI beyond the Celonis documentation"
- "The Process Query Language (PQL) needs improvements -- it has most of the process-related functions but queries from clients cannot be done in PQL"
- "Users need more documentation and examples of PQL language"

### Data Integration Complaints
- "Because the tool is, by its very nature, so data-driven it takes a fair amount of up-front elbow grease to ensure there's not only clean datasets, but also that these can sit in harmony with each other"
- "The process of integrating it to pre-existing programs within the business adds complexity"
- "Data management and the complexity of setting up analysis processes" cited as needing improvement

### Domain Limitations
- "Celonis is heavily attached to financial-based processes (P2P, O2C) and lacks foundational support to domain-based processes such as Banking or Insurance"

## 10. CROSS-VENDOR COMMON COMPLAINTS (ALL PROCESS MINING TOOLS)

### Universal Pain Points Across ALL Vendors

1. **Ease of Use is the weakest dimension** -- Process mining software is most positively evaluated in "Overall" ratings but falls behind in "Ease of Use" consistently (G2 data)

2. **Debugging is painful** -- "Debugging isn't intuitive, and error messages are often unclear, making troubleshooting time-consuming" (Power Automate)

3. **Performance degrades at scale** -- Every major vendor has reviews mentioning slowdowns with large datasets

4. **Learning curve is universal** -- "Teams newer to process mining or automation will want to budget time for enablement" (UiPath); "overwhelming at first" (Signavio); "steep learning curve" (Celonis)

5. **Data preparation is always the bottleneck** -- No vendor has solved the 80% data-prep problem

6. **Enterprise bias** -- All major tools are designed for 1,000+ employee companies; SMBs are afterthoughts

7. **Consultant dependency** -- Most implementations require external consulting; average Process Mining Consultant salary in the US: **$121,288/year**

## 12. MID-MARKET / SMB GAP

**Sources:** [PEX Network](https://www.processexcellencenetwork.com/process-mining/news/processmind-launches-affordable-self-service-process-mining-platform-for-smbs) | [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/search/process-mining-tools/small-business)

### The Problem
- All major process mining platforms (Celonis, SAP Signavio, UiPath) are priced and designed for large enterprises
- "Many interested businesses believe that Process Mining is too expensive, and the time required to get started and prove value is too long"
- If an organization processes **less than a few thousand cases per year**, it's "much more challenging to generate value" from traditional process mining
- Process mining tools are "a bit sophisticated and expensive, making it difficult to implement in smaller businesses"

### Emerging SMB Alternatives
- **ProcessMind** -- Self-service, transparent pricing, no-code, designed for SMBs
- **Apromore** -- Open-source-based, academic origins, lower cost
- **Microsoft Power Automate** -- Most affordable for teams already in Microsoft ecosystem
- **ProM** -- Free/open-source but requires technical resources

### Key Market Gap
The market is bifurcated: expensive enterprise platforms vs. immature/limited SMB tools. There is no strong mid-market solution that combines enterprise capability with SMB accessibility and pricing.

## 14. PROCESS MINING PROJECT FAILURE CAUSES (SYNTHESIS)

### Top 5 Reasons Projects Fail

1. **Data Access & Quality (cited in every source)**
   - Data delays cause projects to stall before analysis begins
   - Incorrect timestamps, noisy data, missing attributes
   - Multiple data sources in different formats
   - 80% of project time spent on data prep

2. **Scope Misalignment -- "The Goldilocks Problem"**
   - Too narrow = no major insights
   - Too broad = unactionable findings
   - "Process mining is a program, not a project" -- Brooks Olphin
   - First insights need to arrive within 4-8 weeks or stakeholders lose faith

3. **Lack of Stakeholder Buy-In (41% cite this -- Deloitte)**
   - Process improvement is misunderstood as a manufacturing-only concern
   - Stakeholders view process mining as "useful but not critical for success"
   - Leads to inadequate resource allocation and unrealistic timelines

4. **Skills Gap (52% cite this -- PwC)**
   - Need process expert + source system expert + data engineer + analytics developer + automation developer + value management professional
   - "It is rare to have all the expertise in the same individual"
   - Increases cost, coordination complexity, and project timelines

5. **Failure to Scale Beyond Pilot**
   - HFS Research: **67% of process mining implementations disappoint**
   - McKinsey: **~70% of transformation programs don't achieve desired outcomes**
   - "Quick win" projects deliver immediate results but leaders struggle to broaden scope
   - 12-24 months for enterprise rollout is typical -- too long for many organizations
